Buyer Education

Think clearly before you buy.

A property purchase involves more than finding a beautiful listing. Consider the property, location, costs, documentation and your own requirements.

01

Define Your Requirements

Start by identifying the things that matter most before beginning a property search.

  • Preferred location
  • Property type
  • Budget range
  • Lifestyle requirements
02

Research the Location

Location can significantly influence the suitability and long-term usefulness of a property.

  • Connectivity
  • Nearby services
  • Neighbourhood character
  • Future considerations
03

Evaluate the Property

Look beyond photographs and consider the property itself during research and visits.

  • Layout and condition
  • Amenities
  • Maintenance considerations
  • Practical suitability
04

Understand the Costs

The displayed property price may not represent every cost associated with a transaction.

  • Financing considerations
  • Transaction costs
  • Maintenance
  • Recurring expenses
