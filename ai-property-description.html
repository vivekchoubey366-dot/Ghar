Better Inputs

Give AI the right details.

The quality of the description depends on the quality and accuracy of the property information provided.

01

Describe the lifestyle

Mention natural light, views, balconies, privacy, entertaining spaces, interiors and other features that help someone imagine living there.

02

Include useful location details

Provide the neighbourhood, major roads, transit, schools, offices, shopping areas or landmarks when relevant.

03

List real amenities

Add only amenities and specifications that actually exist at the property so the generated content remains accurate.

04

Review before publishing

Always verify measurements, prices, specifications, legal claims and location information before using AI-generated copy publicly.
