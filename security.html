Document Handling

Security starts with trust.

A real estate platform can handle sensitive information, from account details and property documents to communications and transaction-related information. GHAR is designed with security as a core part of the platform rather than an afterthought.

Our Approach

Designed for confidence.

GHAR aims to provide a secure digital environment in which users can explore properties, manage their accounts, communicate with relevant parties and continue their property journey.

Security controls may evolve as the platform develops. We continuously work to improve authentication, authorization, infrastructure protection, monitoring and responsible handling of information.

Protection Layers

Multiple layers of protection.

Account Security

Authentication and authorization controls are used to help protect user accounts and restrict access to account-specific functionality.

Authentication

Secure authentication mechanisms help ensure that protected areas of the platform are accessible only to appropriately authenticated users.

Document Protection

Property and account documents should be handled through controlled access mechanisms and appropriate authorization.

Infrastructure

GHAR's application infrastructure is designed with security controls intended to reduce unauthorized access and protect platform services.

Monitoring

Security-relevant activity can be monitored to help identify unusual behavior, operational problems and potential security events.

Secure Development

Security considerations are incorporated into application development, deployment and maintenance processes as the GHAR platform evolves.

Security Practices

Built with responsible controls.

01

Least-Privilege Access

Access to protected platform resources should be limited according to the permissions required for a particular role or operation.

02

Secure Sessions

Authentication sessions are designed to reduce unnecessary exposure and help protect authenticated areas of the application.

03

Data Protection

Information should be collected, processed and retained according to applicable requirements and the purpose for which it is needed.

04

Rate Limiting

Appropriate rate controls can help reduce abuse, automated attacks and excessive requests against application endpoints.

05

Secure Configuration

Platform configuration, secrets and environment settings should be managed separately from public application content.

06

Continuous Improvement

Security is an ongoing process. Controls and practices may be reviewed and improved as GHAR grows.

Protect Your Account

Security is also your responsibility.

Users should take reasonable steps to protect their own GHAR accounts. Never share passwords, verification codes or authentication credentials with another person.

Account Settings
Use a strong and unique password.
Protect your registered device and email.
Enable additional security controls when available.
Never share OTPs or verification codes.
Sign out from devices you no longer use.
Responsible Disclosure

Found a security issue?

Report It Responsibly

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting GHAR, please contact the appropriate GHAR support or security channel with enough information for the issue to be understood and investigated.

Contact GHAR

What to Include

When reporting a potential vulnerability, include the affected page or feature, a clear description, relevant steps to reproduce the issue and any supporting technical information that can safely be provided.

Please avoid accessing, modifying or exposing another person's information.
