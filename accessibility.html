Accessibility Statement

A more inclusive GHAR.

We want everyone to be able to explore properties, understand information, use GHAR services and move through their property journey with confidence. Accessibility is therefore considered as part of the design and development of the GHAR platform.

Our Commitment

GHAR is committed to improving accessibility across its website and digital experiences. We aim to make our interfaces clear, navigable and practical for users with different needs and ways of interacting with technology.

Our accessibility approach is part of our broader commitment to creating a property platform that puts people first.

Accessible Experience

We work toward an experience that supports clear navigation, readable content and interaction across different screen sizes and input methods.

  • Clear headings and content structure.
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Visible interactive controls and navigation.
  • Alternative text for relevant property imagery where appropriate.
  • Keyboard-friendly interaction for supported interface components.
  • Clear labels and understandable interface actions.

Accessibility features

GHAR is designed with practical accessibility considerations throughout the user experience. Features may evolve as the platform develops.

Responsive mobile experience
Keyboard navigation
Readable typography
Strong visual hierarchy
Meaningful image descriptions
Clear interactive controls

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As GHAR adds new property tools, account features, AI services, maps, applications and transaction workflows, we will continue to review how those experiences can be made easier to use.

Some third-party services or external content may have accessibility characteristics outside the direct control of GHAR. Where possible, we aim to identify and address accessibility issues within experiences we control.

Need accessibility assistance?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using GHAR, please contact us. Tell us which page or feature you were using and describe the issue so our team can understand the problem and work toward an appropriate solution.
