Before you invest

A better property decision starts with better questions.

Use GHAR's analysis as a starting framework. Before committing capital, independently verify property documents, title, approvals, market comparables, financing terms, taxes and all transaction costs.

Ask Property Advisor →
01 Verify title

Review ownership and title-related documents through appropriate professional channels.

02 Check approvals

Verify relevant building, development and regulatory approvals where applicable.

03 Compare the market

Compare similar properties and recent transaction evidence rather than relying on one asking price.

04 Calculate total cost

Consider registration, taxes, maintenance, brokerage, financing and other applicable costs.

05 Stress-test rent

Test scenarios with vacancy, lower rent and higher expenses instead of assuming perfect occupancy.

06 Check liquidity

Consider how quickly and at what cost the property could potentially be sold.
