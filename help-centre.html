GHAR AI

Use the GHAR registration journey to create your account. Select the appropriate role when requested and provide the required information.

  • Open the registration or Get Started page.
  • Select the relevant account role.
  • Enter the requested information.
  • Complete the available verification steps.

Check that the account credentials you entered are correct. If you cannot remember your password, use the password recovery journey available from the login page.

  • Check your email or mobile details.
  • Try the password recovery option.
  • Complete any requested verification.
  • Contact GHAR if access remains unavailable.

Use GHAR property search to explore available listings and narrow the results according to your requirements.

  • Open the property search experience.
  • Enter a location or other search criteria.
  • Review available property listings.
  • Open a listing for more information.

Open the relevant property listing and use the available enquiry or contact action. Depending on the listing workflow, you may also be able to request a property visit.

  • Open the property listing.
  • Review the available property information.
  • Submit the available enquiry action.
  • Use the visit workflow where available.

Sellers can begin the property listing process through the GHAR seller journey. The listing process may request property details, images and supporting information.

  • Sign in to the appropriate account.
  • Open the seller property-listing journey.
  • Enter the requested property details.
  • Submit the listing for the applicable workflow.

Submitted listings can proceed through the applicable GHAR property review and approval workflow. The exact status and requirements depend on the listing process.

Keep your property information and supporting documents accurate and available if additional information is requested.

Search GHAR listings for properties available for rent or lease and review the information provided for each listing.

  • Open the property search experience.
  • Use the appropriate rental or lease filter.
  • Review available listings.
  • Submit an enquiry where appropriate.

When a GHAR workflow requests supporting documents, use the upload control provided within that workflow.

  • Use the requested file format.
  • Check that the document is readable.
  • Upload the correct document.
  • Wait for the upload or submission to complete.

Some GHAR workflows may require documents to support an application, property process or access request. Provide accurate documents and follow the instructions displayed in the relevant workflow.

Payment status should be checked through the relevant GHAR account or transaction workflow. Keep your transaction information available if you need assistance.

  • Check the relevant account area.
  • Review the transaction status.
  • Keep the transaction reference if available.
  • Contact support if the status requires clarification.

Review the GHAR refund policy for the applicable terms and conditions relating to refunds and eligible transactions.

Open Refund Policy →

Keep your account credentials private and use the official GHAR account and authentication workflows.

  • Do not share passwords or verification codes.
  • Use a secure password.
  • Review unexpected account activity.
  • Report suspicious activity through the appropriate support channel.

GHAR AI is intended to assist with property discovery, search assistance, property information and other intelligent platform features made available through GHAR.

AI-generated information should be reviewed carefully, particularly when making important property, financial or legal decisions.

Where the listing supports visits, use the available visit-request workflow to provide the required information and preferred timing.

Visit availability and confirmation depend on the applicable property and platform workflow.
