What GHAR AI Considers

More than just square feet.

Location

Micro-market characteristics, connectivity, neighbourhood quality, nearby infrastructure and location demand can influence property value.

Property Size

Built-up area, carpet area, plot dimensions and configuration can materially affect an indicative valuation.

Property Type

Apartments, villas, independent houses, plots and commercial properties may have different valuation characteristics.

Property Age

Construction age, maintenance, renovation history and remaining useful life can affect the estimated market position.

Amenities

Parking, lifts, security, clubhouse, open areas, furnishing and other amenities can influence buyer preferences.

Market Signals

Indicative demand, comparable properties and available market information can help produce a more useful estimate.
