Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GHAR may collect, use, protect and manage information associated with your use of the platform.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We May Collect

  • Account and registration information.
  • Contact information such as email and phone number.
  • Property listing and search information.
  • Saved properties, favourites and comparisons.
  • Visits, offers, applications and support interactions.
  • Documents submitted for supported verification workflows.
  • Payment and subscription information processed through applicable payment providers.
  • Device, browser, security and technical information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide platform functionality, authenticate users, manage listings, process workflows, improve search and recommendations, provide support, detect abuse, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

3. Property and User Information

Information associated with property listings may be displayed to other users when necessary to provide the requested service. Sensitive or restricted information should only be exposed according to applicable permissions and platform controls.

4. Documents and Verification

Documents submitted through verification or document-vault features may contain sensitive information. GHAR should use appropriate access controls, encryption and retention policies for such information.

5. AI Processing

Information may be processed by GHAR AI features to provide search, recommendations, estimation, document assistance, content generation or other requested functionality. Specific AI integrations may involve third-party infrastructure subject to applicable agreements and policies.

6. Payments

Payment credentials should generally be processed by authorized payment providers rather than stored directly by GHAR unless specifically required and appropriately protected.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

GHAR may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics, security and platform functionality. See the Cookie Policy for additional information.

8. Data Security

GHAR should apply appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

9. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers, technology partners, verification providers, payment processors, legal authorities where required, or other parties when necessary to provide a requested service or fulfill a lawful obligation.

10. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, legal requirements, dispute resolution, security and legitimate business requirements.

11. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the service involved, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent or other processing choices.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact GHAR through the platform's support or contact channels.
