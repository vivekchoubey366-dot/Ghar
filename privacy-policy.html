01

Privacy at GHAR

GHAR is designed to provide a modern real estate experience for people looking to buy, sell, rent or explore property opportunities.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected through the platform and the general ways that information may be used to provide, maintain, improve and secure GHAR services.

Our approach

GHAR aims to collect information that is relevant to providing its services and to handle such information responsibly.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GHAR, different categories of information may be provided to or collected by the platform.

Information you provide

  • Name and contact information.
  • Account registration and profile information.
  • Property preferences and search requirements.
  • Property listing information supplied by sellers or authorised users.
  • Information submitted through enquiries, applications, forms or support requests.
  • Documents or other information submitted as part of a property-related process.

Technical information

The platform may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information, operating system, IP address, approximate usage information, pages visited and interaction data.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve the GHAR platform and to provide services requested by users.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide property discovery and search features.
  • Help users find properties based on their preferences.
  • Facilitate communication between relevant parties.
  • Process enquiries, applications and requests.
  • Support property visits and related workflows.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Develop and improve GHAR features, including technology-assisted property discovery.
04

Information Sharing

GHAR may need to share relevant information with service providers, technology providers or other parties where necessary to operate requested services.

For example, information may be shared where necessary to facilitate a property enquiry, property transaction workflow, payment process, communication, document handling, technical infrastructure or customer support.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or legitimate governmental request.

Important

GHAR does not intend this policy to authorise unnecessary disclosure of personal information. Sharing should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

05

Cookies & Similar Technologies

GHAR may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors interact with the platform, maintain sessions and improve the user experience.

Depending on your browser and device settings, you may be able to control or restrict certain cookies through your browser.

06

Data Security

GHAR takes reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, secure infrastructure, monitoring and other safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the services provided.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take appropriate steps to protect their accounts, passwords and devices.

07

Data Retention

GHAR may retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements and meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with your GHAR account.

  • Request access to certain personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Ask questions about how information is handled.
  • Withdraw certain permissions where applicable.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal limitations.

09

Children's Privacy

GHAR's services are intended for users who are legally able to use the relevant services.

GHAR does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

10

Changes to This Policy

GHAR may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, technology, business practices or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version may be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11

Contact GHAR

If you have a privacy question, request or concern, you can contact GHAR through the platform's available support channels.
