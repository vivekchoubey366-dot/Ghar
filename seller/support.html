Frequently asked questions

Common seller questions and quick answers.

Open the seller listing flow, enter the property information, upload the required documents and submit the listing for approval.
A property can remain pending while GHAR reviews listing information, documents, verification data or other required details. Check the property approval page for the latest status.
Buyer enquiries and lead activity are available from your seller leads section. You can review lead details and continue eligible conversations through the connected seller workflow.
Use the seller Visits page to review scheduled visits, upcoming appointments and relevant property information.
Open Seller Offers to review buyer offers and manage the next step in your negotiation workflow.
Seller payment information is available through the Payments section, subject to the transaction and payment records available in your GHAR account.
