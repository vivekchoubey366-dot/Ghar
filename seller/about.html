ABOUT GHAR

A connected platform for modern property selling.

GHAR is designed to bring the major stages of a property journey into one digital ecosystem. For sellers, that means a structured environment for creating property listings, managing information, handling buyer interactions and following transaction activity.

Instead of managing property information across multiple disconnected channels, sellers can use their GHAR workspace as the central point for their property activity.

OUR APPROACH

Make property selling simpler and more transparent.

Our seller experience is built around three principles: clear property information, organised workflows and better visibility into buyer-side activity.

The goal is not simply to publish a listing. It is to create a complete digital workflow around the property.
