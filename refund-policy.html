Refund Policy.

This Refund Policy explains how GHAR handles eligible refunds, cancellations and payment-related requests associated with services provided through the GHAR platform.

Last Updated: October 2026

Refund Policy Overview

GHAR aims to provide clear information about payments, cancellations and refund eligibility before a user completes a transaction. Refund eligibility may depend on the particular service, transaction, subscription, booking or third-party service involved.

01

Eligible Refunds

A refund may be considered where a payment has been incorrectly charged, duplicated, cancelled under an applicable cancellation policy, or otherwise qualifies for a refund under the terms presented to the user before payment.

Where a specific GHAR service contains its own refund or cancellation terms, those terms may apply to the relevant transaction.

Duplicate Payment Duplicate charges for the same eligible transaction may be reviewed for refund.
Eligible Cancellation Certain cancellations may qualify according to the applicable service terms.
Payment Error Payment processing errors may be investigated and corrected where appropriate.
Approved Request Requests approved after review may be processed through the original payment method where possible.
02

Payments That May Not Be Refundable

Not every payment made through the GHAR platform is automatically refundable. Certain services, fees, bookings or completed transactions may be subject to non-refundable terms.

Examples may include:

  • Services that have already been fully delivered.
  • Fees specifically identified as non-refundable before payment.
  • Third-party services whose provider does not permit refunds.
  • Transactions where a refund request is outside the applicable cancellation period.
  • Charges resulting from a user's failure to follow the applicable service requirements.
Important The exact refund terms shown during checkout or service enrollment should always be reviewed before completing payment.
03

Cancellation Requests

Users may request cancellation of an eligible service through the relevant GHAR account, service interface or by contacting GHAR support.

A cancellation request does not automatically guarantee a refund. GHAR may review the request against the applicable service terms, transaction status and cancellation conditions.

Cancellation review may consider:

  • Date and time of the original payment.
  • Date and time of the cancellation request.
  • Whether the service has already been used or delivered.
  • Any applicable cancellation window.
  • Specific terms displayed at the time of purchase.
04

Refund Processing

Once a refund has been approved, GHAR may initiate the refund through the original payment method where supported by the applicable payment provider.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in a user's account may depend on the payment processor, bank, card issuer or other financial institution.

Refund status

Users may be asked to provide transaction information so that GHAR can locate and verify the relevant payment.

Payment provider timelines GHAR cannot guarantee the exact date on which a payment provider or bank will credit an approved refund to the user's account.
05

Third-Party Payments and Services

Some services, listings, transactions or payment processes may involve third-party providers.

Where a third party controls the payment, booking, service or refund process, the third party's applicable terms may also apply.

GHAR may assist users in communicating with the relevant provider where appropriate, but cannot guarantee a refund controlled entirely by an independent third party.

06

Exceptions and Special Circumstances

GHAR may review exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis. Providing information about an exceptional circumstance does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

Additional verification or supporting documentation may be requested where necessary to investigate a refund request.

Fraudulent or unauthorised transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorisation, contact GHAR promptly and also notify your bank or payment provider where appropriate.

Need help with a refund?

If you have a question about a payment, cancellation or refund request, contact the GHAR support team with the relevant transaction information so that the request can be reviewed.
