Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the general framework for cancellations, refund requests and paid GHAR services.

Last updated: October 2026

1. General Principle

Refund eligibility depends on the specific product or service, the transaction terms displayed at checkout, applicable law and whether the service has already been substantially provided.

2. Subscriptions

FREE No subscription payment is charged for the free plan.
BUYER PLUS Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the subscription terms presented during purchase.
SELLER PRO Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the subscription terms presented during purchase.
AGENT PRO Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the subscription terms presented during purchase.
BUSINESS Business plans may have separate commercial terms, billing periods and cancellation conditions.

3. Cancellation

Where subscription cancellation is available, users may cancel through the applicable account or billing controls. Cancellation may prevent future renewals but does not necessarily create a refund for a billing period that has already begun.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe you were charged more than once or charged an incorrect amount, contact GHAR support with the relevant transaction information so the payment can be investigated.

5. Failed Transactions

A failed or reversed payment may be automatically handled by the payment provider. If money was deducted but the transaction did not complete, the payment provider's settlement and reversal process may apply.

6. Property Transactions

Payments between buyers, sellers, tenants, landlords, agents or other parties may be governed by separate agreements. A platform subscription refund policy does not automatically determine the terms of an independent property transaction.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds may be returned through the original payment method where technically and legally possible. Processing time may depend on the payment provider and banking system.

8. How to Request a Refund

Contact GHAR support with your registered account information, transaction reference and reason for the request. Do not send passwords, OTPs or full payment credentials.

9. Changes

GHAR may update this policy when its products, subscription plans, payment infrastructure or applicable requirements change.

Important: Before accepting real customer payments, configure this policy with the actual commercial terms, applicable Indian consumer/tax requirements, payment gateway rules and professional legal advice.
