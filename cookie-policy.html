Your privacy matters.

GHAR uses cookies and related technologies to help provide a secure, reliable and useful platform. This policy explains the general categories of cookies that may be used and the choices available to users.

01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that websites may store on a device when a user visits a website. They can help a website remember information, maintain sessions and understand how services are being used.

GHAR may also use technologies similar to cookies, including local storage, pixels, tags and related identifiers where appropriate.

02 Types of Cookies

Depending on how the GHAR platform is configured, different categories of cookies may be used.

Essential

These technologies may be necessary for core website functions such as authentication, security, navigation and session management.

Preferences

These may help remember selected preferences such as settings, language or other interface choices.

Analytics

Analytics technologies may help GHAR understand traffic, usage patterns and performance so the platform can be improved.

Marketing

Where applicable and permitted, certain technologies may be used to understand campaign performance or provide more relevant content.

03 Why GHAR Uses Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for several purposes, including:

  • Keeping users signed in and maintaining secure sessions.
  • Protecting the platform against fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Remembering preferences and user interface settings.
  • Understanding how users interact with GHAR.
  • Improving page performance and platform functionality.
  • Measuring the effectiveness of features and communications.
  • Supporting relevant content and experiences where permitted.

04 Third-Party Technologies

Some GHAR services may depend on third-party providers that use cookies or similar technologies. These providers may include services used for analytics, security, maps, authentication, communications, payment processing or other platform functionality.

Third-party technologies are governed by the respective provider's policies and terms. GHAR does not treat this Cookie Policy as replacing those third-party policies.

05 Managing Cookie Preferences

Depending on your device, browser and the features available on GHAR, you may be able to control cookies through browser settings or a cookie preference mechanism.

Blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect website functionality. In particular, disabling essential cookies may prevent authentication, security features or other core services from functioning correctly.

Cookie Preferences

Review or adjust available cookie preferences where GHAR provides a cookie-management control.

06 Cookie Retention

Some cookies may remain on your device only for the duration of your browsing session, while others may remain for a longer period depending on their purpose and configuration.

Retention periods may vary based on the technology, service provider, security requirements and user preferences.

07 Changes to This Policy

GHAR may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, technology, legal requirements or operational practices.

When material changes are made, GHAR may provide additional notice where appropriate.

08 Contact GHAR

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how cookies are used on the GHAR platform, you can contact GHAR through the official contact channel.

For broader information about privacy and the handling of personal information, please review the GHAR Privacy Policy.
