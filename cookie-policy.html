Cookie Policy

Learn how GHAR may use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the platform.

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may also be used to remember sessions, preferences, security information and usage patterns.

2. Types of Cookies

Type Purpose
Essential Authentication, security, sessions and core platform functions.
Preferences Remember selected settings and user preferences.
Analytics Understand platform usage and improve performance where enabled.
Security Help identify suspicious activity and protect accounts.

3. Authentication Cookies

GHAR may use secure session mechanisms to maintain authenticated sessions. Disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features from working.

4. Analytics

Where analytics services are enabled, they may collect aggregated or technical information about platform usage.

5. Third-Party Technologies

Integrated services such as maps, payments, analytics, communication or other infrastructure providers may use their own technologies according to their policies.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow users to control cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies can affect authentication and other core functionality.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when GHAR introduces new technologies or changes how cookies are used.

8. Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact GHAR through the official support or contact channels.
